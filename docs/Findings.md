# HR Attrition Analytics - Key Findings

## Workforce Overview

- Total Employees: 1470
- Employees Left: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%

## Department Insights

- Sales recorded the highest attrition rate.
- Human Resources also experienced elevated attrition.
- Research & Development had the largest workforce population.

## Job Role Insights

- Sales Representatives experienced the highest attrition.
- Laboratory Technicians showed significant employee turnover.
- Sales Executives were among the most affected groups.

## Overtime Insights

- Employees working overtime showed higher attrition.
- Overtime appears to be a major factor contributing to employee exits.

## Age Insights

- Employees aged 26–35 represented the largest attrition segment.
- Younger employees showed higher turnover risk.

## Salary Insights

- Employees in lower salary bands experienced higher attrition.
- Attrition generally declined as salary levels increased.

## Recommendations

1. Review workload and overtime policies.
2. Focus retention initiatives within Sales.
3. Improve career growth opportunities for early-career employees.
4. Review compensation strategies for lower income bands.
