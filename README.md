# HR Attrition Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition patterns using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

The objective is to identify key drivers of employee turnover and provide actionable insights for HR teams to improve employee retention and workforce planning.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition impacts:

- Hiring costs
- Productivity
- Employee morale
- Organizational stability

This project helps answer:

- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which job roles are most affected?
- Does overtime influence attrition?
- How do salary and age impact employee turnover?

## Key Findings

- Overall Attrition Rate: **16.12%**
- Sales had the highest department attrition rate.
- Sales Representatives experienced the highest job-role attrition.
- Employees working overtime showed significantly higher attrition.
- Lower salary bands experienced greater employee turnover.
- Employees aged 26–35 represented the largest attrition segment.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Reduce excessive overtime dependency.
2. Improve retention programs within Sales teams.
3. Strengthen employee engagement initiatives.
4. Review compensation strategies for lower salary bands.
5. Focus retention efforts on early-career employees.
